UtangMate Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · App: UtangMate (com.utangmate.app)

The short version: UtangMate does not collect, send, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you write in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Names you enter for the people in your notebook
  • Utang entries: notes, amounts, interest, due dates, and payments
  • Your PIN, saved only as a scrambled (hashed) value, never as the digits themselves
  • App settings, such as whether reminders and fingerprint unlock are on
  • A record of unlock attempts, used to slow down PIN guessing

This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not run servers, and we have no way to see it.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email, or phone number is required
  • No access to your phone contacts, photos, or location
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising

Fingerprint unlock

Fingerprint checks are handled by Android. UtangMate only receives a "matched" or "not matched" answer. Your fingerprint is never seen or saved by the app.

Notifications

Due-date reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through the internet. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Copying and sharing

When you copy a reminder message, it goes to your phone's clipboard. Reminder banner images are made on your phone and only leave it when you choose an app to share them with. If you paste or share into another app, such as Messenger, that app's privacy policy applies.

Device backups

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, your phone may include app data in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and Google, not by UtangMate.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Notebook to erase all records, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

UtangMate is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page and in the app with a new effective date.
